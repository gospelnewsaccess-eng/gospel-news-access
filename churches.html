How an entry gets here

Two ways only:

  1. The church asked to be listed, through the form.
  2. We took it from the church’s own public page.

We never publish a personal mobile number, a home address, or an email address that we worked out from a pattern. A guessed address that bounces burns that address permanently, and it is rude.

What the confidence labels mean

LabelMeaning
Confirmed current Checked within the last 90 days. The date and the method are shown on the entry.
Likely current From a public source we trust, but not re-checked within 90 days.
Unverified No contact details are shown at all — only the name and location. We will not send you to a door we have not checked.

Corrections and removal

If something here about your church is wrong, or you want the entry taken down, tell us and we will fix or remove it. We do not require a reason.

A dedicated inbox for this is being set up. Until it is live we are not printing an address here, because an address that nobody reads is worse than none.

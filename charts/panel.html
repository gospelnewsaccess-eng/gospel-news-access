The GNA Reporter Panel

These are the radio stations, syndicated shows, streaming programmers and DJs who voluntarily report their weekly spin counts to Gospel News Access. Their reporting is 40% of every chart position.

We name every reporter publicly. A chart whose panel is secret cannot be checked by anybody, and a chart nobody can check is just an opinion with numbers on it.

The panel is being built now

No reporters have joined yet, so none are listed. We are not listing stations that have not agreed to report — naming a station that never signed up would be a lie about our own chart.

This page fills in as real reporters join.

Who can join

Gospel and Christian radio stations, syndicated programmes, streaming programmers, and working DJs who can report an honest weekly spin count.

What reporting involves

  • One weekly report of what you actually played and how many times.
  • Submitted by Thursday 11:59 PM Pacific, covering Friday through Thursday.
  • Your station is named publicly on this page, with its market and tier.

What it costs

Gospel News Access does not accept payment for chart consideration, chart position, or reporter panel membership. Any offer to buy placement will be declined and may be reported.

Panel membership is free and always will be. Nobody buys their way onto this page, and no reporter receives anything of value for reporting.

How tiers are set

Tier is assigned on measurable reach alone — never on relationship, never on payment. The weighting is published in full on the methodology page.

Falsified reports

A falsified spin report ends participation permanently, and the removal is stated publicly on this page. The charts are only worth something if the numbers behind them are real.

A dedicated inbox for this is being set up. Until it is live we are not printing an address here, because an address that nobody reads is worse than none.

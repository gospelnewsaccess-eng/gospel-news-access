Proven in Crisis

When the fires came, the doors opened.

As fire moved through Pasadena and Eaton, the ministry team offered practical help alongside messages of hope, faith and encouragement. Families displaced and uncertain found both material support and people willing to sit with them.

The church partnered directly with local authorities and the National Guard, providing its parking lot free of charge to station up to fifteen National Guard vehicles — turning church property into staging ground for first responders working the fire line.

Disaster response is not a program a church adds. It is what a church is for.

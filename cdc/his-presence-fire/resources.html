Help, and where to find it.

A working directory for anyone in Los Angeles who needs assistance — whether or not they ever walk through our doors. Updated as services change.

In immediate danger? Call 911. For a mental health or suicide crisis, call or text 988, free and 24/7. For any other urgent need in Los Angeles County, 211 connects you to a live person at any hour.

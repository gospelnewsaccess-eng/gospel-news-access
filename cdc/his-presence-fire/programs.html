Programs

Five departments.
One point of entry.

Modelled on the approach proven in South Los Angeles since 1994: meet the immediate need, then build the capacity that prevents the next one.

Glendale, California · Wikimedia Commons · CC BY 4.0
Department 01

Disaster Response

Proven under fire, literally.

Rapid mobilization

Volunteers and facility deployed when the region is hit.

First responder support

Facility and staging made available to agencies working the line.

Displaced family care

Practical assistance and presence for households in crisis.

Coordination

Working alongside local authorities rather than around them.

Angeles National Forest · Wikimedia Commons · CC BY 1.0
Department 02

Community Services & Outreach

Immediate need, met without precondition.

Food assistance

Support for households facing food insecurity.

Emergency assistance

Help at the moment of crisis, before it compounds.

Utility assistance

Keeping the lights on while a family stabilises.

Care and connection

Someone to talk to, and somewhere to be sent next.

Pasadena City Hall · Wikimedia Commons · Free licence
Department 03

Housing & Shelter

Keeping families housed, and helping them buy.

Housing navigation

One-to-one help understanding options, applications and deadlines.

Homebuyer education

The path to ownership, taught start to finish rather than assumed.

Displacement support

Immediate help for households displaced by disaster or eviction.

Referral network

Direct connection to the agencies with authority to act.

Angeles National Forest · Wikimedia Commons · CC BY 2.0
Department 04

Financial Wealth Building

Wealth is built on information most people are never given.

Credit counseling

Understanding, repairing and protecting a credit profile.

Homebuyer seminars

Group sessions covering the full purchase process.

One-to-one counseling

Private sessions on budget, debt and savings.

Small business support

Guidance for people starting or steadying an enterprise.

Department 05

Youth & Leadership

Reaching young people before the system does.

Leadership development

Structured mentorship for young people at a turning point.

Academic support

Tutoring, GED and diploma pathways.

Job skills workshops

The practical skills an application actually requires.

Training and placement

Preparation followed by a real route to work.

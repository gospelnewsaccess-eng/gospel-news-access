Gospel News Access Community Development Center is a community development center serving Los Angeles, Faith Community, Media & Workforce. Built on the intersectional model proven by church-based development corporations in Los Angeles since 1994, the center addresses housing, financial capability, community services, youth development and workforce readiness through a single point of entry. A community development initiative of Gospel News Access.

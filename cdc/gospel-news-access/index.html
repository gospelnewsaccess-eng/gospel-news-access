Why This Exists

The gospel community has always had the story. What it lacked was the platform.

Churches, choirs, ministries and artists across Los Angeles generate news every week that never reaches a wire. Meanwhile the young people in those same congregations want careers in media and have no visible path into one.

The established community development model empowers low-to-moderate income communities through an intersectional approach — social services, economic development, housing and healthcare under one roof. This center applies that same logic to the area the model never fully covered: media and narrative.

A community that cannot publish is a community that gets described by others.

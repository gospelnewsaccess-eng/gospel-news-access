About Gospel News Access

Gospel News Access is an independent gospel, Christian and faith news wire. We aggregate the faith world’s reporting, we produce our own original reporting, and we publish our own music charts.

Who owns this publication

Gospel News Access is a property of Global Access Dominion Holdings LLC, which operates publicly as Global Access Holdings.

Who runs this publication

Terry Bryant — Senior Editor

Terry Bryant is a credentialed journalist. He holds international press affiliations and United Nations-connected affiliations.

How the wire works

Most of what you see on the front page is a news wire: headlines from other publications, gathered automatically every 30 minutes. When you click one, you go to the publication that reported it. The photograph shown is the publisher’s own image, credited to them, displayed from their servers. We do not copy it, re-host it, crop out a credit or put our name on it. This is the same model Google News and Flipboard use.

Our own reporting is always clearly bylined as Gospel News Access and lives on our own pages.

Our editorial standards

  • We do not invent anything — not a quote, a source, a statistic, a church, a programme, an event or an outcome. Not once, not to fill a gap.
  • Every original story carries a real dateline and a real timestamp.
  • Where there is no named reporter, the byline is a desk byline — the Gospel Desk, Gospel News Access, or Gospel News Access Staff. We do not invent a journalist’s name to put on a story.
  • A story about a named individual’s conduct requires either primary documentation or an on-record source, and a genuine attempt to reach that person for comment before we publish. If we have neither, the story does not run.
  • When we get something wrong we correct it openly, on the story and on our corrections page. We do not quietly edit and pretend it never happened.

Money

Gospel News Access does not accept payment for chart consideration, chart position, or reporter panel membership. Any offer to buy placement will be declined and may be reported.

We also do not accept payment for news coverage. If we ever carry advertising or sponsored material it will be labelled as such, plainly, on the item itself.

How we are built

This site is deliberately simple: plain HTML served as static files, updated by scheduled scripts rather than by a person clicking publish. The code that fetches the news, extracts the photographs and calculates the charts is open for anyone to read. Our chart method is published in full on the methodology page.

Privacy & ads

Privacy, cookies and advertising

What Gospel News Access does and does not know about you, how advertising on this site works, and how we label anything someone paid for. In plain English. Last updated November 22, 2026.

The short version

Gospel News Access is a news website. There are no reader accounts and no logins, and we do not sell personal information. When advertising is running on the site, Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads. Anything a client paid for is labeled.

Advertising and cookies

We may show ads from Google and its advertising partners. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or to other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

  • How Google uses this information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.
  • You can turn off personalized advertising at any time in Google’s Ads Settings, and opt out of many other vendors’ personalized ads at aboutads.info.
  • Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google’s consent message before personalized ads are shown.
  • You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site still works without them.

We do not place third-party ads inside the text of sponsored articles or clients’ press releases.

Affiliate links

Some links to products, tickets, music or services may be affiliate links. If you buy something through one, we may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. Affiliate links are marked rel="sponsored" in the page code and disclosed on the page where they appear. A commission never decides what we cover or how we cover it.

Sponsored content is always labeled

Anything a client paid for says so where you can see it: sponsored articles carry a “Sponsored” label, banners say “Advertisement”, press releases are labeled as press releases, and paid social posts carry #ad. Advertisers never direct our news coverage. Details for advertisers are on our Advertise page.

Photographs, video and links out

Headline cards from other outlets display that outlet’s own photo from its own servers, so when a page loads, that publisher’s server can see your IP address, the same as if you visited their site. Embedded videos come from YouTube; once you press play, YouTube’s privacy policy applies to that playback. When you follow a link to another publication, you are on their site under their privacy policy.

Forms

When form submission is switched on, we use what you send only for the purpose you sent it: to consider your music, to list your church, or to answer you. Contact details supplied through a form are never published and never sold. You may ask us to delete anything you have sent, and we will.

Hosting

This site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub keeps its own server logs, as any web host does; see GitHub’s privacy statement for what those contain.

Children

This is a general news site. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Questions and changes

Questions about this policy or an ad you saw here: message Gospel News Access on Facebook or Instagram, or see our contact page. If this policy changes in a way that matters, the change is dated here.

Kirk Franklin Opened the Ebony Power 100, Danced With Debbie Allen and Scored Netflix's Christmas Musical. Gospel's King Is Having a Week

He opened Monday's gala with "Revolution," pulled Debbie Allen into a dance the internet keeps replaying, and his music fills "The Gospel of Christmas," streaming now. Which Kirk Franklin song carried you through this year?

LOS ANGELES, California · November 11, 2026 at 4:50 AM Pacific · By the Gospel Desk, Gospel News Access

Kirk Franklin came to the Hollywood Palladium to sing. He left with the dance clip everybody is sending to their group chat.

On Monday night, November 9, Franklin opened the 2026 Ebony Power 100 Gala in Los Angeles with his song "Revolution," according to Ebony's announcement of the night, carried by PR Newswire. Tisha Campbell hosted. Jay-Z, Megan Thee Stallion, Alicia Keys and Keke Palmer were among the honorees.

The dance everybody is talking about

Somewhere between the songs, Franklin and Debbie Allen, honored that night in Ebony's Artist in Residence category, broke into a dance together. The Shade Room posted the clip, and fans have not stopped laughing and replaying it since.

Our GNA family knows this already: gospel was never meant to sit still. "Praise him with the timbrel and dance" (Psalm 150:4).

The album behind the moment

The joy has a soundtrack. Franklin released "I Am What I Am" on October 30, his first double album, with seven studio songs and seven live recordings, according to iHeart and the album's release notes.

The studio side reaches far past church walls: Jelly Roll on "Free Me Tonight," GloRilla on "This Time," Nigerian superstar Davido on "Hope In You" and Tobe Nwigwe on "It Gets Better." The live side goes straight back to the choir stand, with "It Is Well" and Karen Clark Sheard on "Thank You For Everything." DancehallMag reports "Hope In You" has debuted on a Nigerian chart.

The Associated Press described the album as Franklin no longer apologizing for his musical choices. He told The Christian Post he is "older" and "freer now," and in recent interviews he has spoken openly about family regrets and about learning to live from God's grace instead of fear.

On screen and on stage

Franklin produced the music for Tyler Perry's "The Gospel of Christmas," streaming on Netflix now, with Fantasia and Tank leading a cast full of gospel legends. PhillyVoice reports he will also perform at Philadelphia's holiday tree lighting with Kristin Chenoweth.

Why it matters

For three decades Franklin has carried gospel into rooms that did not expect it. This week he did it in Hollywood's biggest Black excellence celebration, on the world's biggest streaming service and on a Nigerian chart, all at once.

The Grammy nominations arrive Monday, November 16. GNA family: which song from "I Am What I Am" is on repeat in your house?

A Family Celebration the Community Embraced: SKVLK Fest Team Says Stabbing Reports Are False

Jamie Foxx's daughter Anelise marked her 18th birthday with a festival that drew families and young people from across the region. The festival's publicist says the reports of a stabbing never happened. How does your church guard young people's stories online?

THOUSAND OAKS, California · November 10, 2026 at 11:00 AM Pacific · By the Gospel Desk, Gospel News Access

Anelise, the daughter of Jamie Foxx, turned 18 with a celebration the whole community could share. SKVLK Fest, the Halloween music festival she created, filled the family's Thousand Oaks home on Saturday, November 7 with carnival rides, music and families. Organizers say more than 25,000 people attended; the Ventura County Sheriff's Office estimated 3,000 or more.

"It was amazing," said Terry Matari Bryant of PR STARPOWER, the festival's publicist, who attended. "The community loved it, the children loved it, and Jamie and Anelise put on a fantastic show."

By Sunday, a darker story was spreading online. TMZ, citing law enforcement sources, reported that a guest had been stabbed, and many outlets repeated it. The festival's team says that is false. "No one was stabbed," PR STARPOWER said in an official statement. According to the statement, two teenagers had a brief scuffle and one received a minor scratch, and Ventura County Sheriff's deputies responded. The festival's team has asked every outlet that published the stabbing claim to correct it.

UPDATED Nov. 10: The Ventura County Sheriff's Office has now confirmed it. In its official release (report RB# 26-146404), the Sheriff's Office says detectives spoke with the young man on November 9 and "determined the victim was not assaulted with a knife, and the injury appears to be from a mutual combat / simple battery." The release also describes two other altercations that night, which remain under investigation. Sheriff's release: local.nixle.com/alert/12829667

WHY IT MATTERS. Scripture warns against bearing false witness, and a rumor about young people can follow them for years. Whatever happened between two teenagers that night, they deserve privacy and prayer, not a viral headline. And a young woman who invited her whole community to celebrate with her deserves to have that story told, too.

Full statement: prstarpower.com/statement-skvlk-fest-2026.html

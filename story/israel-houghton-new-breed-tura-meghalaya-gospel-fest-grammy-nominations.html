Israel Houghton Takes Worship to Northeast India in Garo Attire, Then Heads to the Grammy Nominations

Israel & New Breed closed Gospel Fest 2026 with a stadium night of praise in Tura, Meghalaya, with the state's chief minister in the crowd. Next Monday, Houghton helps reveal the 2027 Grammy nominees. Have you ever worshipped in another language?

TURA, Meghalaya, India · November 11, 2026 at 4:35 AM Pacific · By the Gospel Desk, Gospel News Access

Israel Houghton has led worship on almost every continent. On Monday night, he did it in Garo attire.

Houghton and New Breed closed Gospel Fest 2026 with an evening of praise and worship at the P. A. Sangma Stadium in Tura, in India's northeastern state of Meghalaya, on November 9, according to Meghalaya Monitor and Hub Network.

Hub Network reported that Houghton and his band wore traditional Garo cultural attire for the finale, which also showcased Garo culture.

A state that sings

Meghalaya Chief Minister Conrad K. Sangma was in the crowd. Meghalaya Monitor reported that he praised the people's participation and spoke about the church's role in nurturing the state's love of music.

The Shillong Times reported earlier this month that the state government was planning gospel concerts across the Khasi and Garo Hills, with the Garo Hills event organized together with the Baptist Church. India Today NE reported that the chief minister inspected the preparations in Tura himself.

Meghalaya is one of the few states in India where most people are Christian, and its churches are known for their choirs. A Grammy-winning worship leader filling a stadium there is a picture of how far the gospel travels.

"After this I beheld, and, lo, a great multitude, which no man could number, of all nations, and kindreds, and people, and tongues" (Revelation 7:9).

Next stop: the Grammys

Houghton now heads to music's biggest morning. The Recording Academy has named him among the presenters who will reveal the 2027 Grammy nominations on Monday, November 16, alongside artists including Olivia Dean, Pharrell Williams, Herbie Hancock, Kehlani, Juanes and Carly Pearce, according to grammy.com and the Gospel Music Association. The full list posts at 9 a.m. Eastern on grammy.com.

Israel & New Breed won Best Contemporary Christian Music Album at this year's Grammys for their Spanish-language album "Coritos Vol. 1."

Why it matters

Gospel is not just an American sound. From Tura to Buenos Aires to Lagos, believers are singing the same songs this week in their own languages. GNA family, where in the world would you love to worship with believers from another culture?

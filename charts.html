The GNA Charts

Four weekly charts, measured by Gospel News Access. These are our own charts — our own measuring instrument. They are not derived from, licensed from, or dependent on any other publication’s chart.

How these charts are built · Who reports to us · Submit your music

No chart has published yet

The chart engine is built, tested and scheduled — it runs every Tuesday at 12:00 noon Pacific. It has not yet published a chart because it has not yet received a full tracking week of data.

We will not publish an invented chart to fill this space. A chart with no measurement behind it is not a chart. What appears here will be the real result of real reported spins, real streaming movement and real release activity.

Two things start the clock: radio and streaming programmers joining the reporter panel, and artists and labels submitting music.

Gospel News Access does not accept payment for chart consideration, chart position, or reporter panel membership. Any offer to buy placement will be declined and may be reported.

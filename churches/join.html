What we publish

Only what you enter below, and only what a church would normally put on its own website: the church name, city, state, country, denomination, public website, public service times, public social handles, and your senior pastor’s name as your church itself publishes it.

What we never publish

  • A personal mobile number.
  • A home address.
  • Your email address — we use it to confirm the listing, and that is all.
